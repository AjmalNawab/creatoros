# How Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts distribute content from new and small accounts (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-24. Labels: **[OFFICIAL]** means Meta or YouTube said it. **[DATA]** means a third-party study with a stated method. **[CREATOR]** means creator, vendor or anecdotal claims. Claims dated before 2025 are marked **(older)**.

## Facebook Reels: official ranking signals, unconnected distribution, and why a new Page gets almost no impressions

### Takeaway
Meta's published Reels system card ranks Reels by predicted watch time, completion, skip/hide and shares (WhatsApp and Messenger sends are named explicitly), plus topic match and "whether the reel is original content." Unconnected (non-follower) recommendation is a core candidate source. Since March 2026 Meta has also been deprioritising "unoriginal" Reels and can make an account "non-recommendable." Meta publishes nothing about a "new Page testing phase." A Reel with 3-5 impressions suggests it is barely entering the unconnected recommendation pool. It is not evidence of a sample that tested badly.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Reels candidates come from accounts you follow, plus "content you may be interested in from creators you may not follow," "reels that are similar to those you've recently interacted with," "reels from people or accounts that are similar to those you've followed or interacted with," and cross-app recommendations from Instagram. A lightweight model first selects "approximately 10-100 of the most relevant reels" for each viewer. Integrity processes then reduce the distribution of problematic content. — [Meta Transparency Center: Facebook Reels AI system](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [OFFICIAL] The predictions the Reels models make include:
  - how long you'll watch
  - whether you'll watch rather than skip
  - whether you'll watch to completion
  - whether you'll like it
  - whether you'll hide it or ask to see less
  - whether you'll start a viewing session or leave
  - whether you'll share it to WhatsApp
  - whether you'll share it via Messenger, and how many people you'll share it with on Messenger

  — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [OFFICIAL] Input signals include:
  - how many times others viewed the reel, how many watched it to completion, how long they typically watched, and how often they skipped it
  - topic match to the topics you watch and click
  - the author, and the authors whose reels you've watched or interacted with
  - the reel's length
  - "Whether the reel is original content"

  The card does not mention new accounts. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)
- [OFFICIAL] Originality policy (13 March 2026):
  - Original means content "filmed or produced directly by a creator or owner of a Profile or Page."
  - Reels that use third-party material can still count if they present "something genuinely new" (information, analysis, storyline).
  - Unoriginal means react, watch-along or stitched clips without added value, narration over existing footage without added value, re-uploads, and "low-value changes" such as borders, captions or speed changes. Unoriginal content "will likely be deprioritized in Feed and Reels."
  - Repeat offenders can be "deemed non-recommendable and demonetized." Creators can appeal.
  - Views and watch time for original Reels roughly doubled in H2 2025 compared with H2 2024.
  - The post says nothing specific about AI-generated content.

  — [Meta Newsroom: Rewarding Original Creators on Facebook](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/)
- [OFFICIAL] Meta's Recommendation Guidelines say recommended content must meet higher standards than content from followed accounts, because users didn't choose to follow it. The guidelines are in the Facebook Help Center. — [Meta: Recommendation Guidelines](https://about.fb.com/news/2020/08/recommendation-guidelines/) (older, 2020)
- [CREATOR] Pages can lose recommendation eligibility through automated classification. Owners can check and request review through Meta Business Suite or the Page support inbox. — [Brewers Association](https://www.brewersassociation.org/brewing-industry-updates/a-digital-health-check-for-your-brewery/), [CellarPass](https://business.cellarpass.com/blog/why-facebook-no-longer-recommends-alcohol-related-business-pages-and-what-you-can-do-about-it-011226)
- [OFFICIAL] Professional Dashboard: the November 2023 Reels metrics added a "distribution score" showing "how your Reel did compared to your other Reels." Meta later made Views the primary metric across content types. (older, 2023) — [Meta: Helping Creators Test Content and Earn Rewards](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/11/helping-creators-test-content-and-earn-rewards/), [Meta for Creators: Introducing Views](https://creators.facebook.com/introducing-views-to-simplify-content)
- [CREATOR] One vendor says the distribution score is driven by retention, 1-minute views, reactions, comments and shares, and is offered only in certain regions. This is not confirmed by Meta. — [Sirency](https://www.sirency.com/blog/facebook-insights-explained)
- [OFFICIAL] Pages vs professional-mode profiles: Meta's comparison lists "in-feed recommendations" for both, "subject to eligibility." Pages additionally get admin roles, Business Suite and Ads Manager. Meta publishes no reach difference between the two. — [Meta for Creators: Pages and profiles](https://creators.facebook.com/tools/facebook-pages-and-profiles)
- [OFFICIAL] Meta's Reels best practices page (2022, older; the page fetched was in Urdu, rendered in English):
  - 9:16 vertical video
  - a strong hook, and a story with a beginning, middle and end
  - text and effects
  - voiceover allowed
  - **1-2 relevant hashtags**; #Reels and #Explorepage "won't help"
  - avoid recycled-looking Reels, other apps' watermarks and low resolution
  - **share each Reel to your Story after posting**
  - cross-post from Instagram with "suggested on Facebook"
  - no posting frequency is given

  — [Meta for Creators: Reels best practices](https://creators.facebook.com/reels-best-practices-tips-tricks)
- [OFFICIAL] Older Meta guidance says creators get "the greatest distribution when they primarily post content that is filmed or created by them," and to aim for "shareworthy" content. — [Meta for Creators: growing your presence](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/best-practices-for-growing-your-facebook-presence)
- [OFFICIAL] Creator Fast Track (18 March 2026) is aimed at established creators from other platforms (US/Canada, later Brazil). It gives boosted Reels distribution and guaranteed pay for 3 months. Requirements: a Page at least 30 days old, and **15 Reels in 30 days across at least 10 different days**. Third-party reports give different payout figures. The program is not available to a 1-follower Pakistan-audience Page, but its cadence requirement shows the volume Meta considers a serious Reels creator. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-meta-creator-fast-track-facebook-creators-pay/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/18/facebook-launches-a-new-monetization-program-to-attract-popular-creators-from-tiktok-youtube/)
- [DATA] Socialinsider, 2026 (25M posts, 130K+ business pages): average Facebook engagement rate is 0.15%. Reels were the only format whose engagement rate rose in Q1 2026 (0.15%), then fell to 0.13% in Q2 while remaining the top format. Reels engagement rate declines as brands grow larger. The study gives no reach benchmark for tiny Pages. — [Socialinsider Facebook benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook)
- [CREATOR] Secondary sources report that Reels discovery targets non-followers, with completion and watch time as key signals. A "UTIS" survey signal and a "+7% organic views Q4 2025" figure appear in vendor posts but not in Meta's own materials. — [Socialpilot](https://socialpilot.co/blog/facebook-algorithm), [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/meta-ranking-signals-for-video-on-facebook/463604)

### Inferences
- Ranking is per viewer: the model scores Reels already in each viewer's 10-100-item candidate set. With no followers and no engagement history, a new Page's Reel rarely enters candidate sets except through "similar to what you interacted with" or topic matching. 3-5 impressions is consistent with near-zero candidate retrieval, not with failing a test.
- Shares into WhatsApp and Messenger are explicit model targets. Sending a Reel's Facebook link (not the downloaded MP4) to genuinely interested people in WhatsApp generates real watch, completion and share signals on the Reel itself, plus "authors you've interacted with" edges. This is legitimate if the people are real and interested. Mass-spamming groups risks hides and "see less" signals, which are negative predictions.
- Risk for Chai & Curiosity:
  - AI narration over painted illustrations is self-produced, so it should qualify as original under Meta's definition.
  - Archive photos with narration could resemble "narrating existing footage" if they dominate the Reel. Keep added analysis and storyline prominent.
  - Never re-upload the same Reel.
  - The owner should check Professional Dashboard, then Monetization or account status / "Recommendations," for any non-recommendable flag or originality notice.
- The Urdu-language market is smaller for topic matching. Clear Urdu captions and on-screen text help topic classification (inference, not stated by Meta).
- Share to Story is an official Meta tip. Its real effect is small with 1 follower, but it costs nothing.

### Gaps
- I found no official Meta statement on a "new Page" testing or sandbox period, or on how many initial impressions a new Page's Reel gets.
- I found no current (2025-2026) official doc defining the Professional Dashboard "Distribution" metric's baseline.
- I found no official statement on hashtags' 2025-2026 effect on Facebook Reels beyond the 2022 "1-2 relevant hashtags" tip.
- I found no official data on posting frequency's effect on Reels distribution for small Pages.
- I found no reliable data on reach differences between a Page and a professional-mode profile.

## YouTube Shorts: feed testing, viewed vs swiped away, engaged views, and why views come only from search

### Takeaway
YouTube says the Shorts feed works by finding an audience: the system shows a Short to some viewers and expands distribution based on their response ("the audience is the algorithm"). Posting volume itself neither helps nor hurts. The March 2025 view-count change did not change recommendations. A new channel whose Shorts get views only from search has not yet been picked up by the Shorts feed. That is common early and is usually broken by more uploads and a stronger "viewed vs swiped away" rate. The 71% "stayed to watch" (viewed vs swiped away) figure is decent but probably not yet strong enough to trigger the feed with no history.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Since 31 March 2025, a Shorts "view" counts whenever a Short starts to play or replays, with no minimum watch time. The old metric continues as "engaged views." YouTube says the change does not affect YPP eligibility or earnings. — [Search Engine Journal, 26 March 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-changes-shorts-view-counts-no-change-to-monetization/543005/)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube's explainer says a view starts at the first frame. Engaged views are taps to watch or watching past the opening seconds. "There's no change to recommendations," and "the vast majority of analytics remain anchored on engaged views." Note: the page as fetched showed a date of 19 August 2026, with an effective date of 24 August 2026. This conflicts with the widely reported 31 March 2025 Shorts change. It may be a later extension of the same view definition to other formats, so verify on the page before quoting dates. — [YouTube Blog: Engaged views explained](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/)
- [OFFICIAL] "Viewed vs swiped away" is the share of viewers who chose to watch a Short versus swiping past it in the feed. It is in YouTube Studio under Analytics, Content, Shorts. — [YouTube Help: Understand your content performance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281)
- [CREATOR] Benchmark ranges such as 70-79% or 80%+ for viewed vs swiped away circulate online, but YouTube publishes no benchmarks. — [Socialinsider Shorts analytics guide](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-guide/), [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate)
- [OFFICIAL] (older, 2023) Shorts product lead Todd Sherman on Creator Insider:
  - Most Shorts views come from the feed, where the viewer makes no deliberate choice.
  - The system "goes and finds a seed audience." Shorts may get a few hundred or thousand views and then stop while the system looks for an audience.
  - Posting more Shorts doesn't itself expand or reduce reach. Lots of low-quality clips can hurt.
  - Re-uploading is "not a great strategy."
  - "The audience is the algorithm."

  — [TechCrunch, 25 Aug 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/), [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/), [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/34020532)
- [OFFICIAL] (older, 2024) Creator Liaison Rene Ritchie: "The algorithm follows the audience, so please the audience. Check your Audience tab." — [Tubefilter, 26 Aug 2024](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/08/26/rene-ritchie-shorts-creator-faqs/)
- [OFFICIAL] The Related Video feature lets a Short link to a long-form video, livestream or another Short on the same channel. — [YouTube Blog: related videos traffic guide](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/)
- [OFFICIAL] (older, 2022) Todd Beaupré: Shorts watch history is kept separate so that it doesn't "give the algorithm the wrong idea" about long-form tastes. The two formats are treated as non-competing, and a later 2022 update began recommending a channel's long-form videos to its Shorts viewers. — [vidIQ, 27 Jul 2022](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-long-form-viewers-algorithm), [vidIQ, Aug 2022](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-recommends-long-form-videos-shorts-viewers)
- [CREATOR] Since about September 2025, older Shorts (more than 30 days old) on dozens of large channels dropped sharply while new uploads were unaffected ("the flattening"). The evidence is anecdotal, and Google made no comment. This suggests the feed favours fresh uploads. — [Tubefilter, 5 Dec 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/12/05/youtube-shorts-algorithm-change-new-uploads/)
- [CREATOR] Titles and descriptions help YouTube understand a Short's topic, especially for Search and surfaces outside the feed. A new channel's audience is not yet understood. — [vidIQ Shorts algorithm 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/), [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/youtube-shorts-not-getting-views/)
- [DATA] Nate Black's analysis of 35B Shorts views, as reported by vidIQ:
  - Channels with at least 200 Shorts tended to see steady view growth.
  - Shorts of 13 or 60 seconds performed best.
  - The first 3 seconds are critical.

  No methodology was published. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- Search-only traffic means the Shorts are indexed and match queries, which is good metadata. The Shorts feed, however, has not yet shown them to a seed audience at scale, or did so only to a handful of viewers with no follow-on.
- Likely reasons:
  - the channel has very few uploads, so there are few chances to be tested
  - no viewer history is tied to the channel
  - English engineering-failure content competes against large incumbents
  - a 71% viewed rate may be middling in this niche (no official benchmark exists)
- The search-only pattern is not a penalty. The usual remedy is consistent volume of fresh Shorts with a stronger first 1-2 seconds (visual hook, no slow intro), aiming to push viewed vs swiped away up and to raise average percentage viewed and loops.
- Because "the flattening" suggests the feed favours fresh uploads, regular new uploads matter more than old ones resurfacing (anecdotal).
- Shorts and long-form on one channel do not penalise each other algorithmically according to YouTube (2022 statements). However, Shorts viewers convert poorly to long-form. Use the Related Video link on each Short to point to the matching long-form video.
- The 71% figure should be read as "viewed vs swiped away" only if that is what the Studio label says. Search viewers chose the Short deliberately, so search-driven viewed rates are naturally inflated compared with feed traffic.

### Gaps
- I found no official 2025-2026 YouTube statement on how many feed impressions a brand-new channel's Short gets, or on a "new channel" test period.
- I found no official benchmark for viewed vs swiped away.
- I could not locate a 2025-2026 Creator Insider video on Shorts and long-form interaction. The official statements found are from 2022-2023.
- The engaged-views blog date conflict (March 2025 vs August 2026) is unresolved.

## Typical timelines and benchmarks for new accounts, and concrete legitimate actions

### Takeaway
The best 2025-2026 large-sample data (vidIQ, 448K channels created in H1 2025) shows the following. A typical new channel's Shorts median about 1,300 views at 30 days from the first upload onward, with no improvement by the 10th. Long-form starts at a median of about 180 views and improves by about 51% by the 10th upload. Breakouts are a "best of N tries" phenomenon. No comparable large-sample new-Page data exists for Facebook. The levers that unlock initial distribution are volume, consistency, hooks and retention, originality, and real shares.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA] vidIQ study (published 28 July 2026):
  - Sample: channels created 1 January to 30 June 2025 with at least 10 same-format uploads in the first 90 days; 367,243 Shorts channels and 137,355 long-form channels.
  - Median 30-day views by upload position:

    | Upload | Shorts | Long-form |
    |---|---|---|
    | 1st | 1,362 | 180 |
    | 5th | 1,275 | 211 |
    | 10th | 1,344 | 272 |
    | Best of first 10 | 8,832 | 1,948 |

  - Share of channels whose best of first 10 reached 1K views: 86.5% (Shorts) vs 57.8% (long-form). Reached 10K: 48.0% vs 30.8%.
  - Per-video chance of 1K views: 56.6% (Shorts) vs 30.2% (long-form). Chance of 10K: 20.9% vs 10.0%.
  - The study is observational. It covers no cadence or time-to-traction, and channels that quit before 10 uploads are excluded. "Volume buys you more tries."

  — [vidIQ Research: first 10 videos](https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/)
- [DATA] Socialinsider 2026: Facebook average engagement rate is 0.15%, and Reels reached 0.15% in Q1 2026 and 0.13% in Q2. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook)
- [OFFICIAL] Meta's Creator Fast Track cadence (15 Reels in 30 days on at least 10 days) is the volume Meta asks of creators it actively boosts. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/)
- [CREATOR] An AI summary attributes to Rene Ritchie the advice to launch with several (about 4) videos so the system has a watch history, and to treat the first years as "school." Unverified. — [Gist.ly summary](https://gist.ly/youtube-summarizer/youtube-secrets-algorithmic-success-for-new-creators)

### Inferences
Concrete legitimate actions, as synthesis:

**Facebook (Chai & Curiosity)**
1. Check Professional Dashboard for account status / recommendation eligibility and any originality notices. Appeal if flagged.
2. Post consistently: aim toward the 15 Reels / 30 days / 10+ days cadence Meta uses for Fast Track.
3. Front-load a visual hook in the first 1-3 seconds, use burned-in Urdu captions, and use 1-2 relevant hashtags.
4. Use Share to Story on every post.
5. Share the Reel link (not a re-upload) one-to-one or into relevant WhatsApp and Facebook groups where it is on-topic and welcomed. WhatsApp and Messenger sends are explicit ranking targets.
6. Keep content clearly self-produced. Avoid duplicate re-posts and minor-edit reposts.
7. Consider linking an Instagram account and posting there with "suggested on Facebook," since cross-app recommendation is an official candidate source.

**YouTube (Margin of Safety)**
1. Keep uploading fresh Shorts regularly. Breakouts come from many tries, and the feed appears to favour new uploads.
2. Optimise the first second to raise viewed vs swiped away, and track it per Short rather than relying on the search-inflated rate.
3. Keep search-friendly titles, since search is currently the only source working.
4. Add a Related Video link from each Short to its long-form video.
5. Don't re-upload Shorts that underperformed.
6. Watch the Audience tab, per Ritchie.

**Expectations:** Weeks to a few months and 10-30+ uploads before the feed reliably tests the content is consistent with the vidIQ data. This is inference, as no source gives an official timeline.

### Gaps
- I found no credible 2025-2026 dataset on new Facebook Pages' first-month Reels impressions or time to first distribution.
- I found no Buffer, Hootsuite or Metricool 2025-2026 study specifically on new-account timelines in the time available.
- I found no data on Urdu-language or Pakistan-market Reels benchmarks.
