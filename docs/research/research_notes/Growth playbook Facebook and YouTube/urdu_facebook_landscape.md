# Urdu explainer landscape on Facebook and YouTube (Pakistan audience)

Researched 2026-11-24. Channel counts come from third-party trackers (vidIQ, HypeAuditor, Social Blade) and change often; the snapshot date is given where the source states one. No Facebook Page follower counts could be verified through web search: Facebook Pages are not indexed by public trackers the way YouTube is.

## 1. Which Urdu / Hindi-Urdu explainer and documentary channels are big, and what do they publish?

### Takeaway
The verified leader for this format is **Zem TV**: about 6.1M YouTube subscribers, about 1.46B views, roughly one upload every two days. Its biggest hits are curiosity "why/how/secret" explainers on science, engineering and mega-projects, all with **English titles**. Most other names in the brief either couldn't be verified (Dekho Suno Jano, Nuktaa, "Info at Ahmad") or turned out to be different, smaller or off-topic channels (Raaz TV). Pure Urdu *science* channels are small. The large Urdu "education" channels are exam/news portals (ilmkidunya, UrduPoint) or general facts channels.

### Cited Findings
- **Zem TV** (@zemtv.official, Pakistan-based, started 20 Jun 2018): 6.14M subscribers, 1,460,427,266 views, 492 videos as of 24 Nov 2026. It gained 20K subscribers and posted 15 videos in the last 28 days (about one every 2 days) — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zemtv.official/)
- Zem TV's most-viewed videos: "How Einstein's Brain was Different ?" (15.2M), "Deepest Hole in Earth - How Deep We can Dig in Planet ?" (14.72M), "Hidden Secrets of Taj Mahal" (14.62M), "Why Dubai's Man Made Islands are Still Empty" (14.5M), "Largest Oil Rig Manufacture and Transport" (13.09M). Latest upload "Why Has Zamzam Well Never Dried Up?" (22 Nov 2026) reached about 1.13M views in 34 hours — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zemtv.official/)
- Zem TV's growth: 5.46M subscribers in Mar 2025, about 6.04–6.14M in late 2026. Trackers disagree on its category (Entertainment / Lifestyle / Education). Its channel description reads "Interesting facts and figures about the most interesting individuals on earth" — [vidIQ (es)](https://vidiq.com/es/youtube-stats/channel/UCs_ineLBnNTGjrArNzjSLZg); [Vidanalyze](https://vidanalyze.com/channel/zemtv.official)

- **Ecosystem scale:** more than 1,200 Pakistani channels have passed 1M subscribers, more than 19,000 have passed 100K, and more than 140,000 have passed 10K. **Over 60% of watch time on Pakistani-produced content comes from outside Pakistan** (South Asia, the Middle East, Europe, North America). Named education creator: Minoqtopus (long-form video essays) — [ProPakistani, Jul 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/29/google-reveals-pakistans-massive-global-growth-on-youtube/)

### Inferences
- Zem TV's top-5 videos show which topics win in this format: famous-object secrets (Taj Mahal), extreme engineering (deepest hole, oil rig), brains/genius (Einstein), Gulf mega-projects (Dubai islands, NEOM/MBS), and Islamic-heritage science (Zamzam: 1.1M views in under 2 days). Copy the *pattern* (a famous, concrete subject plus an unanswered "why"), not the topics themselves.
- Zem TV titles are in English even though the audience is Urdu/Hindi-speaking, which suggests English titles widen search reach across the Pakistan, India and diaspora audience. Worth A/B testing on Chai & Curiosity: an Urdu headline band in the video plus a bilingual caption.
- Zem TV's cadence (about 15 videos per 28 days) is the realistic benchmark for a leader. A new page can start lower but needs to stay consistent.
- The Urdu *science* niche is under-served at scale: the big Urdu "knowledge" channels are portals or general facts channels, and dedicated science channels are small. That is an opening.
- The jinn, horror and Islamic-story genre is common in Urdu, but Raaz TV's numbers show volume alone doesn't guarantee views.

### Gaps
- Not verified: "Dekho Suno Jano", "Nuktaa", "Mahaaz"-style channels, "Info at Ahmad" and "Factinfo"-type pages. None appeared in searches, so no counts are given (unverified).
- No Facebook Page follower counts were found for Zem TV, UrduPoint or any Urdu facts Page. They need a manual check of each Page in a browser (Page transparency or follower count).
- Typical video lengths for Zem TV weren't available from the trackers. From memory, Zem TV videos are roughly 8–15 minutes, but this is unverified.
- Narration style (human vs AI voice, Urdu vs Hindustani register) wasn't confirmed from sources.

## 2. What hooks and title patterns work in Urdu?

### Takeaway
The only hard evidence available is Zem TV's top-title patterns: **"Why…?" / "How…?" questions, "Hidden Secrets of X", superlatives ("Deepest", "Largest") and failure/downfall framing**. No source quantified Urdu-specific hooks such as "کیا آپ جانتے ہیں".

### Cited Findings
- Zem TV's top titles use these patterns: "How [famous person]'s X was Different?", "Deepest X — How Deep can we…?", "Hidden Secrets of [landmark]", "Why [famous place] is Still Empty", "Largest X", "Why Is [famous] Failing? Downfall of …", "Why Has [sacred/famous thing] Never …?" — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zemtv.official/); [Vidanalyze](https://vidanalyze.com/channel/zemtv.official/videos)
- Third-party Reels guides say completion rate tends to peak at 15–30 seconds, so the hook must land in the first 1–3 seconds (general guidance, not Urdu-specific) — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/facebook-reels-time-limit-strategy/)

### Inferences
- Patterns to copy for the headline band and first spoken line:
  1. A "why" question about a famous, concrete thing: "زمزم کا کنواں کبھی خشک کیوں نہیں ہوا؟"
  2. A superlative: "دنیا کا سب سے گہرا گڑھا…"
  3. Hidden secret / "nobody tells you": "تاج محل کے چھپے راز"
  4. A famous person plus "different": "آئن سٹائن کا دماغ مختلف کیوں تھا؟"
  5. Failure or downfall of something big.
- "کیا آپ جانتے ہیں…" is a generic opener. A specific question with a concrete noun is probably stronger, judging by the title evidence, but this is untested.
- The 43 s average watch on a 4-minute video means viewers left early. Put the answer's tease in the headline band and the payoff structure (setup, then "but here's the strange part") within the first 10 s.

### Gaps
- No study or creator interview measured Urdu hook phrasing (for example "کیا آپ جانتے ہیں" vs question headlines). This needs the page's own A/B tests.

## 3. Best Reel length and posting times for Pakistan (PKT); Facebook user stats 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Facebook reaches about **53M (DataReportal, late 2025) to 70.6M (NapoleonCat, Oct 2026)** people in Pakistan. The audience is about 75–80% male and the largest group is aged 25–34. Facebook now treats all uploads as Reels with no length cap, but **boosting only works for Reels of 90 s or less**. No Pakistan-specific posting-time study exists. The best proxy is the national **7–11 pm evening internet peak**, plus global Reels data pointing to 8–11 pm.

### Cited Findings
- NapoleonCat (Meta ad-audience data): **70,628,301 Facebook users in Pakistan in Oct 2026 (29.4% of population)**. 75% men, 25% women. Largest group: **25–34, at 40.8%** — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-pakistan/). Other months reported: 69.4M (Aug 2026), 66.0M (Sep 2026); the monthly figures are volatile — [NapoleonCat via search](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/facebook-users-in-pakistan/2026/06/)
- DataReportal Digital 2026 Pakistan (published Nov 2025, Oct 2025 data): **117M internet users (45.6%)**; **79.9M social media identities (31.2%)**, up 25% in a year. **Facebook ad reach 52.9M** (20.6% of population, 45.3% of internet users; 79.2% male / 19.5% female; +15.5% year on year). **YouTube ad reach 54.3M** (60.3% male / 39.7% female). Instagram 22.4M. Median mobile download speed 24.32 Mbps (+24%) — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan)
- DataReportal's TikTok row in the fetched output showed 79.9M (18+), identical to the total social figure, so it may be a parsing error. Treat it as unverified — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan)
- Digital 2027 Pakistan: 94.1M social media identities in Oct 2026 (36.1% of population) — [DataReportal 2027](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-pakistan)
- **Reel length:** Meta removed Reels length and format restrictions; posts made in the app or on the web become reels "from a 30-second clip to a 20-minute-plus episode". The Reels Publishing API still accepts only 3–90 s. Several sources date the change to June 2025 — [Meta Creators blog](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/making-it-easier-to-create-videos-on-facebook); [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/how-long-can-a-facebook-reel-be/)
- **Boosting works only on Reels ≤90 s.** A third-party guide says completion peaks at 15–30 s — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/facebook-reels-time-limit-strategy/)
- **Posting times:** there is no Pakistan-specific study. Timetopost lists 3–5 pm PKT but admits it uses a global curve with an editorial baseline. Global Reels data: DashSocial reports 9 pm as the best Reels hour and 8–11 pm as the strongest weekday window; SocialBu suggests 6–8 pm — [Timetopost](https://timetopost.co/best-time-to-post/facebook/pakistan/); [DashSocial](https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-facebook); [SocialBu](https://socialbu.com/blog/?p=17912)
- **Usage peak proxy:** Pakistani internet slows nationwide **between 7 pm and 11 pm** from peak-hour congestion — [ProPakistani, Nov 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/11/04/heres-why-internet-slows-down-between-7pm-and-11pm/amp/); PTA acknowledges severe peak-hour congestion in dense areas — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistan-denies-firewall-behind-4g-disruptions-as-users-complain-of-slow-internet-2616727). During Ramadan the pattern shifts (4G slowest between midnight and 9 am) — [ProPakistani 2023](https://propakistani.pk/2023/03/15/how-ramadan-affects-mobile-network-experience/amp/)
- Average mobile data use rose from about 6GB to 8GB per user per month in about a year; 2025 total 14,153 PB — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/10/06/how-much-mobile-data-does-an-average-pakistani-use-every-month/)
- **Monetisation context:** Meta replaced in-stream ads, Ads on Reels and the Performance bonus with unified content monetisation (reported threshold 300K views in 28 days). Pakistani creators have faced payout and bank-account restrictions since Mar 2025. Facebook Stars has been available in Pakistan since Dec 2022 — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/03/17/facebook-quietly-tightens-monetization-policy-in-pakistan/); [ARY](https://arynews.tv/facebook-lowers-monetization-threshold-for-new-creators); [Samaa](https://samaa.tv/2087330633-facebook-updates-monetization-policy-for-pakistani-creators); [ProPakistani 2022](https://propakistani.pk/2022/12/09/meta-launches-new-way-for-facebook-creators-to-earn-money-in-pakistan/)

### Inferences
- Default test schedule: **post at about 8:30–9:30 pm PKT** (after Isha, inside the 7–11 pm national peak), with a second test slot at 1–2 pm (lunch break) or 4–5 pm. Fridays and Sundays are worth testing. After about 2–3 weeks, switch to the Page Insights "when your followers are online" data.
- During Ramadan, shift to after Taraweeh / around sehri (late night).
- Length: the 43 s average watch on 4 min suggests making **45–90 s the main format** (it can be boosted and suits the completion curve). Keep 2–4 minute vertical pieces for proven topics only, and structure them as a series of re-hooks every 20–30 s.
- The audience is skewed: about 75–80% male, mostly 25–34. Topics and voice (male narration) fit this audience.
- Over 60% of Pakistani YouTube watch time comes from abroad, so diaspora time zones (Gulf at PKT −1, UK at PKT −5) add a secondary evening peak. Gulf evenings overlap PKT 8–11 pm well.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan-specific Facebook engagement-by-hour dataset was found. PTA's Q3 2025 OpenSignal report may hold hourly data — [PTA PDF](https://www.pta.gov.pk/assets/media/2026-02-10-3490d48f-eMobile-Network-Experience-Benchmarking-Report-Q3-2025.pdf) (not opened).
- No data on posting frequency for Pakistani Facebook explainer Pages.

## 4. Reels vs long video on Facebook in Pakistan; does Hindi competition matter?

### Takeaway
There is no direct measurement of the Reels-vs-long split for Pakistan. Facebook has merged all video into Reels, so the distinction is now mostly about length. Hindi-language channels share the same spoken language and recommendation pool, but no data quantifies their Pakistani audience share.

### Cited Findings
- All Facebook video uploads become Reels (June 2025 change) — [Meta Creators blog](https://creators.facebook.com/blog/making-it-easier-to-create-videos-on-facebook)
- Social media users in Pakistan grew 25% in a year. Facebook ad reach grew 15.5% while YouTube ad reach fell 2.9% (late 2025) — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan)
- TikTok is running a STEM-content push aimed at young Pakistanis (ProPakistani, Aug 2025; not opened) — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/08/22/tiktoks-stem-push-is-reshaping-how-young-pakistanis-learn/)
- Hindi creators like Dhruv Rathee (33.2M subscribers) target India-first audiences. One list notes that Pakistani commentators discussing Indian topics "attract more viewers from India than Pakistan" — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@dhruvrathee/index.md); [HypeAuditor India politics](https://hypeauditor.com/top-youtube-news-politics-india/?p=2)
- Over 60% of watch time on Pakistani content comes from abroad, which implies Pakistani creators already compete in the shared Hindi-Urdu pool — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/29/google-reveals-pakistans-massive-global-growth-on-youtube/)

### Inferences
- Hindi "facts" channels compete for the same spoken-language viewers on both platforms. Differentiate with the **Urdu Nastaliq headline band**, Islamic and Pakistani-heritage angles (Zamzam, Pakistani landmarks, Muslim scientists) and Pakistani examples. These are things Hindi channels rarely do.
- Since Facebook is growing in Pakistan while YouTube ad reach is flat, Facebook-first vertical posting is reasonable.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan-specific data on Reels vs long-form views, and no public analytics on Pakistani audience share for Hindi channels.

## 5. Topic demand signals (Pakistan search / trends)

### Takeaway
Google's Pakistan Year in Search 2025 is dominated by cricket, dramas, exam results and government schemes. Its "how to" list is practical (money, phones). The tech list shows strong **AI curiosity** (Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Grok, Google AI Studio). Health questions (diabetes, cholesterol) and floods also rank. No Urdu "why" science query list was found.

### Cited Findings
- Pakistan 2025 Tech trending: Gemini, Tamasha, DeepSeek, myco, On4t, Google AI Studio, Claude, iPhone 17, Grok, NFT — [Dawn Images](https://images.dawn.com/news/1194542)
- Pakistan 2025 "How to": check e-challan Karachi, see unsent Instagram messages, car insurance, invest in crypto, invest in stock market, apply for credit card, make Ghibli photo, choose a lawyer, print from phone, upload a blog — [Dawn Images](https://images.dawn.com/news/1194542)
- Local news trending: Punjab Socio-Economic Registry, **Karachi floods**, Iran, Asaan Karobar Card, **Chenab flood situation**, exam results, PM Laptop Scheme, gold rates — [Dawn Images](https://images.dawn.com/news/1194542)
- Questions about diabetes management and high-cholesterol diets remained high — [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2581212/cricket-dramastop-google-searches-in-pakistan)

- Proven video demand from the Zem TV view counts: Einstein's brain (15.2M), deepest hole (14.7M), Taj Mahal secrets (14.6M), Dubai islands (14.5M), oil rigs (13.1M), Zamzam (1.1M in under 2 days) — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zemtv.official/)

### Inferences
- Topic ideas with demand signals (to verify with the `trend-ideas` playbook and Google Trends PK before production):
  - **AI explainers:** "AI کیسے سوچتا ہے؟", "ChatGPT/Gemini جھوٹ کیوں بولتا ہے؟" (AI tools trending)
  - **Floods and monsoon science:** "کراچی میں بارش سے سیلاب کیوں آتا ہے؟", "چناب میں سیلاب کیسے بنتا ہے؟" (seasonal: Jul–Sep)
  - **Body and health "why":** diabetes, cholesterol, "چائے پینے سے نیند کیوں اڑتی ہے؟" (health questions rank high; fits the "Chai" brand)
  - **Islamic-heritage science:** Zamzam (proven), the Kaaba's construction, the Moon-sighting science behind Ramadan/Eid timing (seasonal spikes)
  - **Mega-engineering and Gulf projects:** NEOM/The Line, Dubai islands, the Karakoram Highway, Tarbela Dam
  - **Famous-mind and superlative topics:** Einstein's brain, the deepest hole, the hottest/coldest places, including Pakistani superlatives (K2, Jacobabad heat)
  - **Smog** in Lahore (Nov–Jan seasonal), heatwaves (May–Jun), earthquakes (after events)
- Timeliness tactic: tie evergreen explainers to Pakistani news spikes (floods, smog, heat, Ramadan moon sighting, cricket-science pieces during big matches).

### Gaps
- No Urdu-language Google Trends or YouTube search data for science or "why" queries (for example "کیوں" queries) was retrievable through web search. It needs a manual Google Trends check (geo=PK) and YouTube autocomplete checks.
