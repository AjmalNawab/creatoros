# YouTube metadata

- **title (≤100 chars):** Urdu first, then ` | ` and short English. Lead with the curiosity hook plus the main keyword. Honest; no clickbait, ALL CAPS, emojis spam, `<` or `>`.
- **description:** 2–4 short Urdu paragraphs. The first 150 characters matter most (shown in search): say what the viewer learns. No links. Chapters, sources and the AI-voice disclosure are appended automatically.
- **chapterTitles:** exactly one per scene, 2–5 Urdu words each.
- **tags (8–15, total <500 chars):** real search phrases, mixing Urdu script, Roman Urdu (e.g. "aatish fishan kaise phatta hai") and English.
- **hashtags (≤3):** e.g. `#سائنس`, `#اردو`, plus one topic tag.
- **thumbnailText:** 2–4 bold Urdu words that are readable at small size. Complements the title rather than repeating it.
- Use the selected idea's keywords if present in the project. Never promise anything the video doesn't show.
