# Script writing

The prompt contains the **brand block** (niche, audience, tone, editorial rules). Those rules are mandatory. The word target comes from the brand voice pace. Stay within ±15%; the validator rejects anything outside ±25%.

## Margin of Safety (English): engineering story structure (5–8 scenes)
1. **Cold open (first 8 s):** drop the viewer into the moment, with a concrete detail and tension. Example: "At 10:30 a.m. the deck began to twist." No "In this video…", no channel intro.
2. **Context:** what was built, by whom, why it was impressive or new.
3. **The warning signs:** what people noticed, and what was missed or dismissed.
4. **The failure or near-miss:** what happened, told plainly; respectful about any loss of life.
5. **Why it happened:** the engineering root cause, explained with one clear analogy and one diagram-able mechanism (forces, resonance, units, fatigue, materials). Attribute findings: "investigators concluded…".
6. **The fix and the lesson:** what changed in design, codes or practice, and why it still matters today.
7. **Outro (1–2 lines):** the takeaway line, then a soft call to action ("subscribe for a new story every week").

**Style:** calm, precise, suspenseful. Short sentences, since each becomes a subtitle line. Use concrete numbers with units (metric first, imperial in brackets if useful). No exaggeration beyond the sources, and no blame beyond official findings.

## Urdu Science Explainers (Urdu)
- Hook in 5–8 s (a question or vivid image). Then one idea per scene: what, then how, then why, plus one everyday analogy. End with a wow fact or a genuine Pakistan angle, then a call to action.
- Natural spoken Pakistani Urdu in Urdu script, with common English loanwords. Spell numbers as words for the TTS.

## Both
- Use only facts from the research sheet (high or medium confidence).
- `visual` per scene (English): one concrete animated diagram in the brand's visual style. No photos, real people or logos.
- If the prompt contains **OWNER FEEDBACK**, address every point; it overrides these defaults.
