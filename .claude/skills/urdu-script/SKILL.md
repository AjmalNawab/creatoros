# Urdu narration script

**Target:** the prompt gives a word count (about 175 words per minute for the Asad voice at +8%). Stay within ±15%; the validator rejects anything outside ±25%.

## Structure (5–8 scenes)
1. **Hook (first 5–8 seconds):** a question or vivid image that makes the viewer stay. Example: «ذرا تصور کریں…» or «کیا آپ جانتے ہیں…؟». Never start with a greeting or channel intro.
2. **Body scenes:** one idea per scene, building understanding step by step (what, then how, then why). Use one everyday analogy (a soda bottle, a pressure cooker, a ball).
3. **Wow fact or local angle:** the most surprising fact, or the genuine Pakistan connection.
4. **Outro:** a one-line recap, then a short call to action (subscribe + suggest the next topic in comments).

## Language
- Natural **spoken Pakistani Urdu** in Urdu script, not textbook or Persianised Urdu. Use common English loanwords (سائنس، کرسٹ، میگما).
- Short sentences: each becomes one subtitle line (aim for ≤ 15 words).
- Spell numbers as words (تیرہ سو پچاس), which the TTS reads correctly.
- Use only facts from the research sheet (high or medium confidence). No exaggeration.
- `visual` (English) per scene: a concrete animated diagram that can be drawn with simple SVG shapes and labels. No photos, real people or logos.

## Revisions
If the prompt contains **OWNER FEEDBACK**, address every point explicitly. Feedback beats these defaults.
