# Research: how rainbows form

A rainbow appears when sunlight shines into falling raindrops while the Sun is behind the viewer. Each drop bends (refracts) the light as it enters, reflects it off the back of the drop, and bends it again as it leaves. Because each colour bends by a slightly different amount, white sunlight spreads into a band of colours, seen as an arc about 42 degrees from the point directly opposite the Sun.

**Audience:** General Pakistani audience, curious families and students (10+), watching on YouTube/Facebook; simple spoken Urdu.

## Avoid
- Saying raindrops 'contain' colours or are coloured; the colours come from sunlight.
- Saying a rainbow is a physical object at a fixed place that can be reached.
- Claiming exactly seven colours exist; seven is a naming convention for a continuous spectrum.
- Myths presented as fact (pot of gold, etc.).
