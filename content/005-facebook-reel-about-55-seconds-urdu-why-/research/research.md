# Research: Facebook Reel, about 55 seconds, Urdu: why a dead person's finger usually cannot unlock a phone fingerprint sensor (and the surprising cases when it can). Platforms: facebook

Most phone fingerprint sensors are capacitive: they read the tiny electrical differences between the ridges and valleys of living skin. After death, blood flow stops, the skin cools, dries and can wrinkle, so the sensor usually cannot read a match, and phones add their own barriers such as asking for the passcode after 48 hours. But it is not impossible: experts say a very recently deceased person's finger can sometimes still work, which is the surprising twist.

**Audience:** Curious Pakistani smartphone users aged 15-35 on Facebook who wonder how everyday tech works

| 5 | Jain also said that if a person has only recently died, it is possible to unlock a phone with their finger. | high | Live Science |
| 6 | Scientists are not sure exactly how long after death the skin stops conducting electricity well enough for a sensor. | medium | Live Science |
