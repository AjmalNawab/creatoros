# Research: Facebook Reel, about 60 seconds, Urdu: why Lahore smog gets trapped every winter (temperature inversion: cold air near the ground under a warm lid, crop-residue burning, traffic and brick-kiln smoke, still winter air) and what actually helps. Hook: a famous Lahore landmark disappearing in smog. Platforms: facebook

Every winter Lahore's smog is not mainly new pollution; it is the same year-round smoke trapped close to the ground. In winter the ground cools fast at night, a layer of warmer air sits above the cold air (a temperature inversion) and acts like a lid, the layer in which smoke can mix and spread shrinks sharply, and winds over eastern Punjab are often very weak. Under this lid, emissions from vehicles, industry and brick kilns build up, and late-season crop-residue burning around Lahore (on both sides of the border) adds a big seasonal burst. In November 2024 Lahore recorded its worst readings ever, and Punjab closed schools across many districts. What helps is cutting the sources (crop burning, dirty vehicles and kilns), because the weather lid cannot be removed.

**Audience:** Lahore and Punjab residents aged 15-35 who live through smog every winter and wonder why it returns each year

## Avoid
- Blaming only India or only farmers; sources differ on the shares and the problem is shared
- Quoting a single record AQI number as exact (monitors disagree: AP said over 1,000, others cited higher local readings)
- Medical advice or specific mask/health claims
- Fear-mongering ('poison air will kill you')
- Calling smog 'fog'; fog is water droplets, smog is pollution (they often mix)
