Wow-fact tone. Use real licensed footage. Platforms: tiktok, facebook

The hottest place on Earth depends on what you measure. Air temperature: the official World Meteorological Organization record is 56.7°C (134°F) at Furnace Creek, Death Valley, USA, on 10 July 1913 (some scientists question that reading; Libya's 58°C El Azizia record was disqualified by the WMO in 2012). Ground (land surface) temperature: NASA satellites (MODIS) measured 70.7°C in Iran's Lut Desert in 2005, the highest in their seven-year survey; a later study suggests the true peaks may be even higher. The ground gets far hotter than the air because dark, dry surfaces absorb sunlight. Pakistan's own Jacobabad is one of the hottest cities on Earth.
