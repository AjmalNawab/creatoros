### Scene 1: Hook (~0:00–0:14)
*Visual: dark screen, then a glowing crack in the ground, then a volcano silhouette lights up*

## English summary (for review)
1. **Hook:** rock under your feet is hot enough to melt; when it finds a way out, a volcano erupts.
2. **Layers:** crust, mantle, magma (700–1,200 °C).
3. **Rising magma:** lighter than rock, collects in chambers, holds gas like a soda bottle.
4. **Eruption:** pressure drops, bubbles form, rocks can't hold it, eruption. Magma becomes lava.
5. **Two types:** sticky magma explodes (Mt St Helens 1980); runny magma flows (Hawaiʻi).
6. **Ring of Fire** (~1,350 possibly active volcanoes); Pakistan's Koh-e-Sultan.
7. **Benefits** (fertile soil, new islands), then subscribe and comment.
