# Research: TikTok short, about 45 seconds, Urdu, series Sach ya Jhoot (Myth or Fact): Is the Great Wall of China visible from space with the naked eye? Reveal the real answer using astronaut statements and NASA public-domain photos, explain why (width vs distance, colour like the ground). Debate-starting, playful tone. Use real licensed footage. Platforms: tiktok, facebook

Myth: you can see the Great Wall of China from space with your own eyes. Fact: from low Earth orbit (about 400 km up, where the International Space Station flies) it is generally NOT visible to the naked eye, and it is certainly not visible from the Moon. The wall is long but narrow, and it is built from stone and earth of a similar colour to the land around it. China's first astronaut, Yang Liwei, said in 2003 he could not see it. NASA astronaut Leroy Chiao photographed it from the ISS in 2004 with a 180 mm lens, but said he could not see it when he took the picture. ESA's Alexander Gerst photographed it in 2018 with an 800 mm lens and said it was next to impossible with the naked eye. A few astronauts (Eugene Cernan, Ed Lu) said it may be visible from lower orbit under very favourable conditions.
