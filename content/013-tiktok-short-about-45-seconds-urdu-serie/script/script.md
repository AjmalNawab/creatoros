# Script: TikTok short, about 45 seconds, Urdu, series Sach ya Jhoot (Myth or Fact): Is the Great Wall of China visible from space with the naked eye? Reveal the real answer using astronaut statements and NASA public-domain photos, explain why (width vs distance, colour like the ground). Debate-starting, playful tone. Use real licensed footage. Platforms: tiktok, facebook

**Title options**
1. سچ یا جھوٹ؟ دیوارِ چین خلا سے نظر آتی ہے

3. دیوارِ چین اور خلا: بچپن کی سب سے بڑی غلط فہمی
