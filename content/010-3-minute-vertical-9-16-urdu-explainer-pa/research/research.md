# Research: 3 minute vertical 9:16 Urdu explainer : Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali flies on China's Shenzhou-24 to the Tiangong space station, the first foreign astronaut to visit it and the first Pakistani on an orbital mission. Who he is, how the launch, orbit and docking work, the Pakistani university experiments, life on the station, and how he returns. Publish only after the launch is confirmed. Platforms: facebook

Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is part of the three-person Shenzhou-24 crew with commander Tang Hongbo and flight engineer Wu Yi. The launch is scheduled for 25 November 2026 at 08:18 Beijing time (05:18 Pakistan time) on a Long March 2F rocket from the Jiuquan launch centre, with docking to the Tianhe core module of China's Tiangong space station planned about 3.5 hours after reaching orbit. He would be the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong and the first Pakistani on an orbital mission (Namira Salim flew a suborbital Virgin Galactic flight in 2023). He came out of a China-Pakistan astronaut agreement signed in early 2025, a selection in which two Pakistani candidates (Ali and Khurram Daud) trained in Beijing, and he flies as a payload specialist focused on experiments, including biotechnology experiments from Pakistani universities. He is expected to stay about a week and return to Earth with two Shenzhou-23 crew members, while one Shenzhou-23 astronaut stays for a year-long mission. NOTE: the launch had not happened when this was researched; confirm launch and docking before publishing.
